Summary & Future Work
// what we built, what we proved, where this goes

AFib detection from heartbeat timing alone — personalised, explainable, and watch-ready.

✓
98.9% discrimination accuracyTested on 24 patients the model had never seen — none of their data was used during training. Beats all compared methods.
✓
Respects individual differencesBy learning only within-patient comparisons, the model doesn't confuse "Patient A's normal" with "Patient B's normal" — crucial for real-world generalisation.
✓
A score you can actually explainEvery prediction is a 0–1 number on the patient's personal SR→AF scale. No black box — doctors can see how close someone is drifting toward AFib.
✓
Runs on a smartwatchOnly needs heartbeat timing gaps as input — from hospital ECG or a consumer smartwatch. Small enough to run on-device with no internet connection.

// Future Work

  • Live testing on consumer smartwatches and fitness rings
  • Validate across multiple hospitals and patient populations
  • Predict AFib onset hours ahead by tracking risk score trends
  • Clinical pilot: ambulatory screening programme for at-risk adults
  • Shrink model further for microcontroller deployment (Cortex-M)
