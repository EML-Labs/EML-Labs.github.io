RR Interval Calculation
// the heartbeat timing signature

The gap between heartbeats — the RR interval — tells us if the rhythm is healthy or not.

  • RR interval — the time (in milliseconds) between two consecutive heartbeat peaks
  • Healthy heart — intervals are similar to each other, with only small natural variation
  • AFib heart — intervals jump around wildly with no predictable pattern, like a shuffled playlist
  • These timing patterns are rich enough for AI to tell SR from AFib — no bulky waveform needed
  • Privacy-first — we only store timing numbers, not your raw heartbeat waveform
