11 · Methodology
Our Approach
// how our AI model works — step by step

Our model learns your personal rhythm first — then flags when it starts going wrong.

01

Input — 200 Heartbeat Gaps

We feed the model a list of 200 back-to-back beat timing gaps (RR intervals). This tiny snapshot — from an ECG or smartwatch — is all it needs.

02

Pattern Finder — Convolutional Neural Network

A CNN scans the rhythm at multiple scales simultaneously — like reading a paragraph word-by-word, sentence-by-sentence, and paragraph-by-paragraph at once — to spot subtle irregularity.

03

Training Trick — Patient-Aware Learning

When training, we only compare rhythm segments from the same patient. This stops the model from confusing "Patient A with SR" and "Patient B with SR" — each person's normal rhythm is unique.

04

Output — Personal AFib Risk Score

For each new patient, the model defines their personal "normal ↔ AFib" direction and assigns a risk score from 0 (healthy) to 1 (AFib) for every 200-beat window. Continuous, interpretable, real-time.
